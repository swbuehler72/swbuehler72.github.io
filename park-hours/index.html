Theme park hours, November and December 2026

Daily opening and closing times for all four parks, taken from Disney's official Hours & Events Calendar. Disney posts 60 days ahead, so December 31 is not available yet. Hours are subject to change, so check its calendar again before you go.

EEH: Extended Evening Hours Early close for ticketed event

Early Entry and evening hours

Early Entry opens 30 minutes before every opening time shown. Extended Evening Hours (EEH) run 9 to 11 PM at EPCOT and 6 to 8 PM at Animal Kingdom on the marked dates, for guests of Disney Deluxe Resorts and other select hotels.

Event nights

  • Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party runs 7 PM to midnight at Magic Kingdom through December 22. The park closes to day guests at 6 PM on those nights.
  • Disney Jollywood Nights runs 7:30 PM to 12:30 AM at Hollywood Studios through December 23. The park closes to day guests at 7 PM on those nights.
  • Both need a separate ticket. The EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival runs daily through November 21.

Source

Walt Disney World Hours & Events Calendar, checked for every posted date on October 31, 2026.
