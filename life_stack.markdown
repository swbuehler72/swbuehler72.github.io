A _life stack_ consists of the tools, apps, services, and other things that one uses to manage his or her work and life.  This one is mine. You can read some other ones [here](https://aaronparecki.com/life-stack) and [here](https://www.johnpe.art/life-stack/), and there are numerous others across the web.

> Most of the product links are to the U.S. Amazon store; I do NOT receive any commission or affiliate consideration.

## Work Stack

There is not much to say here about my work setup, because that's almost entirely dictated by my employer for data security and privacy reasons, but I can indicate the following:

- [HP ZBook Firefly G8](https://support.hp.com/us-en/product/details/hp-zbook-firefly-15.6-inch-g8-mobile-workstation-pc/2100188570) workstation laptop (i7, 32GB RAM, 512GB SSD).
- Microsoft stack (Office, CoPilot, company internal apps).

The rest of the setup is listed in my personal stack.

## Personal Stack

My personal stack is intentionally different and is almost entirely Apple-based. 

- 14&Prime; M5 [MacBook Pro](https://apple.com/macbook-pro) • 24 GB RAM, 1 TB internal storage. I've previously owned a MacBook Air and multiple Mac minis. My Apple history goes back to the iBook G4, and even further back to the Mac Plus in the high school computer labs (and I did a state word processing competition using AppleWorks on an Apple IIe).

- [ViewSonic VX3418-2KPC](https://a.co/d/04ewPmHZ) ultrawide display • 34&Prime; 3440 &times; 1440 @ 144 Hz HDR, with more than enough inputs for everything.

- [Canon PIXMA TR160](https://a.co/d/08FlVc20) portable inkjet printer (I downsized from an HP OfficeJet Pro because I simply don't print a whole lot of things).

- [Ricoh ScanSnap ix2500](https://a.co/d/097YRQ2h) Document Scanner (I _do_, however, scan and parse _everything_ that comes through on paper, thus ScanSnap's flagship scanner).

## Mobile Stack

- 13&Prime; M5 [iPad Pro](https://apple.com/ipad-pro) • 128 GB, with Magic Keyboard folio and Apple Pencil Pro. 5G from my wireless carrier.

- [iPhone 17 Pro Max](https://www.apple.com/iphone-17-pro/) • 1 TB (because I make a lot of photos and video). I use my anytime upgrade to update to the latest iterations as they're released, always the Pro Max because of my long fingers.

## Media (Photo/Video) Stack

- Nikon CoolPix P1100 bridge camera (125&times; optical zoom, 24&ndash;3000 mm 135 equivalent).
    - Filters: UV, CP, ND4, and ND100000 (that last one for solar photography).

- [Insta360](https://insta360.com) [X5](https://a.co/d/0aeXGsWo), [X4](https://a.co/d/0cHirv00), and [X3](https://a.co/d/05wFTnuP) 360° video cameras.

- Insta360 ONE R modular camera (5.4K 360° and 4K ultrawide modules). (obsolete)

- [Sirui AM-404FL](https://a.co/d/03J2s1jH) 75.8&Prime; monopod with QC-55 quick-release head. Rarely extended its full length because (a) Disney Parks has a strict 6&prime; tripod height limit and (b) stability becomes a factor.

- Joby selfie stick with screw-on tripod base (52&Prime; max height with the base, which makes it a monopod).

- Insta360 lightweight monopod (43&Prime; max height)

- A couple of 45&Prime; Insta360 selfie sticks that I never use because most places either frown upon their use or ban them outright, and I don't like it when other people use 'em so I lead by example.

- Manfrotto 60&Prime; medium-duty tripod, which is out on the balcony for recording launches.

- A couple of [Cambofoto 73&Prime; tripods](https://a.co/d/0aYXSLe7) with monopod inserts.

- [ULANZI Super Clamp](https://a.co/d/0euKBwWP) camera mount.

- [Insta360 Flow 2 Pro](https://a.co/d/0i1G3vPI) mobile AI gimbal, which I don't use as often because being seen with one makes everyone think you're a snooty livestreamer, which Disney Parks has started to heavily frown on because they've become a distraction to other guests (particular after [one got SWATted](https://lamag.com/crimeinla/police-swarm-disneyland-after-swatting-call-linked-to-livestreamers/) while live from Disneyland Resort).

- Various random no-name chest, head, wrist, hand, dash, and clip mounts.
